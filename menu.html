Treatment Spa

60–90 min

Designed specifically for hair care, this spa is ideal for colour-treated, permed, chemically straightened or keratin-treated hair. Recommended as regular maintenance to help keep your hair healthy, smooth and beautifully conditioned.

Including:
  • 01 — Hair Consultation
  • We assess your hair’s current condition, including dryness, damage, breakage, texture and previous chemical services. Based on what your hair needs on the day, we select the most suitable products and treatment approach for you.
  • 02 — Personalised Shampoo
  • Your shampoo is selected according to your hair’s condition and treatment history. Whether your hair is colour-treated, damaged, chemically treated or keratin-treated, we choose the most suitable professional shampoo for your individual needs.
  • 03 — Micro Mist Treatment
  • Using the Takara Belmont Micro Mist system, a fine warm mist surrounds the hair while the treatment is processing. This creates an ideal treatment environment and allows us to take a more considered approach than simply applying a hair mask for a few minutes at the basin.

  • Your treatment is selected and applied according to the condition of your hair. Depending on what your hair needs, we may focus on repair, moisture, strength, smoothness or manageability — with particular attention to damaged mid-lengths and ends.
  • 05 — Professional Finishing
  • We complete the service with carefully selected leave-in care, heat protection and finishing products, followed by a full blow-dry and light styling. This allows you to see and feel the condition, softness and manageability of your hair after the treatment.

FAQ

Booking & pricing

What's included, what isn't, and how to choose between the treatments.

Which treatment should I choose?

Start with what is bothering you. If it is the scalp — itchiness, oiliness, flaking, tightness, or simply wanting the full ritual — book the Dreamin' Head Spa. If it is the hair itself — dryness, damage from colour or heat, breakage, ends that will not behave — book the Treatment Spa.

Both are priced by hair length and both finish with a full blow-dry. A cut can be added to the head spa for $40. Not sure? Call us before you book and we will match the treatment to what your hair and scalp actually need.

Is the blow-dry really included, or is it an extra?

Included, always — on every head spa service, at no extra charge. The finish is part of the treatment, not an add-on. You leave styled and ready, never with wet hair.

Every price shown is in Australian dollars and includes GST. There are no surprises at the counter.

What is the Treatment Spa, and do I need it?

It is our hair-focused session: consultation, a cleansing shampoo, then an intensive treatment carried deep into the hair by the Takara Belmont micro mist system, and a protected blow-dry finish. It is a treatment in its own right, not a mask left on for a few minutes at the basin.

It is the one to book if your hair is dry, chemically treated or heat-damaged. If your concern is the scalp rather than the hair, the head spa is the better choice — and if you are unsure, we will tell you which one your hair actually needs at the consultation.

How do I book, and what if I need to change my appointment?

Book online in under a minute using the Book Now button, or send us a message. We work by appointment only, Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00am–6:00pm.

As each booking reserves the room and your practitioner exclusively, we ask for at least 24 hours' notice for changes or cancellations.
